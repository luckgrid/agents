# pr

Draft a pull request description. Do not open or push it.

Include:

- what and why
- each commit (short subject, why it exists)
- checks run against the exact candidate revision, with relevant output; label omissions, redactions and home-path normalization explicitly
- what was not checked
- review rounds and dispositions when they exist

Keep private identifiers and secrets out of public text; retain exact evidence in its permitted authority. A prior result at another revision does not validate the current candidate. In the guest, `workbench` owns promote and PR creation for the owner.
