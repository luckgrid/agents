# Record

Create a dated record when an episode, discovery, review, or session needs a
durable account. Preserve what happened and how it is known.

- Use the episode's start date in the filename. Choose a `kind` that describes
  the record's role, such as `session`, `research`, `review`, `incident`, or
  `evidence-log`.
- Separate source material and direct observations from interpretation. Say
  who or what supplied evidence and when; mark inference, uncertainty, and
  unverified claims as such.
- Preserve owner-provided evidence and historical facts verbatim when the
  template requires it. Quote owner words only when asked or when the source
  record itself quotes them; otherwise summarize faithfully and attribute.
- Keep accepted decisions in decision records, proposals labeled proposed,
  delivery/status in its report, and current position in STATE. A record of
  runtime activity is not durable public evidence by itself.
- Remove private paths, credentials, account or agent identifiers, addresses,
  and other sensitive runtime details before writing to a public wiki. Do not
  paraphrase private evidence into an identifying form.
- Add stable frontmatter and links to the relevant decision, source, and STATE
  pages. Do not create a new record by copying another page's ID.

If the episode captures a decision, use [decide](decide.md) as well. For a
session transcript or chat, use [crystallize](crystallize.md).
