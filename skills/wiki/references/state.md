# State

`records/STATE.md` is the wiki's single hand-kept register of current position.
Update it when verified work changes status, a gate opens or closes, or the
next action changes.

- Read the underlying record, decision, or delivery evidence before changing a
  status line. Distinguish completed work from proposed, queued, blocked, and
  in-progress work.
- Keep each entry concise and link to its evidence. A delivery report proves
  what was delivered; it does not establish an accepted policy unless a
  decision says so.
- Name open gates and the next concrete step. Do not present a hypothesis or
  stale status as current fact.
- Do not copy mutable status into other pages. Link them to STATE instead.
- Do not rewrite historical records to make them match current status. Record
  corrections or later events and update STATE to point to them.

If evidence is incomplete or contradictory, preserve that uncertainty and
leave the status unresolved. Use [record](record.md) for new evidence and
[decide](decide.md) when the owner makes a decision.
