# Decide

Use a decision record only for an actual owner decision, not a recommendation,
working assumption, or proposed path.

1. Confirm the owner's intent and the relevant context. If scope or meaning is
   unclear, ask rather than choosing for them.
2. Record the owner's words only when supplied as a quote or explicitly
   requested. Otherwise give a faithful, attributed summary and date.
3. State the durable rule separately from the evidence of the choice. Include
   scope, consequences, and any conditions or exceptions needed to apply it.
4. Give the record a stable ID and the next appropriate decision filename and
   number according to the wiki's convention. Link its sources and affected
   architecture or STATE pages.
5. Keep a proposed decision marked proposed until accepted. Once accepted, do
   not revise its substance; add a superseding decision if the rule changes.
   Mechanical fixes such as links and formatting may follow the wiki template.
6. When accepting a lasting rule that belongs in this wiki's architecture or
   foundation pages, propagate it into those pages in the same change (or in
   the immediate follow-up on the same branch) with a link to the decision's
   stable ID as canonical provenance. Do not leave a newly accepted durable
   rule only in the decision file. Decisions accepted before this rule need
   not be backfilled unless a later task edits them; the requirement is
   procedural for new acceptances, not a historical rewrite mandate.

Do not infer approval from a draft, silence, delivery, or a test result. Keep
the discussion or evidence record separate and link it to the decision.
